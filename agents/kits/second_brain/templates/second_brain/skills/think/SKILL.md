# Structured Thinking

Derived from `claude-obsidian` v1.9.2 (MIT); adapted for vibe-engineering.

Separate observations, assumptions, alternatives, risks, and the chosen conclusion. Read relevant vault context before re-deriving prior decisions, and cite the pages that informed the result.

Offer a `save` capture only when the conclusion is durable. This skill does not authorize external research, writes, or automation by itself.
